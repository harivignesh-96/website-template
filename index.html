The Craft

Woven where
silk meets stone.

In Kanchipuram, a soft silk saree takes a weaver around ten days on the loom. The body and border are woven apart and interlocked by hand (korvai), so the border never frays away from the body.

  1. 01Mulberry silkPure silk yarn, dyed in sandal and maroon.
  2. 02ZariSilver-core thread, gilded for the border and pallu.
  3. 03KorvaiBorder interlocked with the body by hand.
  4. 04Soft finishLight weight that drapes easily.
